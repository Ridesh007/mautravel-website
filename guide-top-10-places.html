Top 10 Places to Visit in Mauritius

Pictures, MAUTRAVEL ratings, visiting time, best days to visit, and local planning advice for the island's most memorable stops.

The Mauritius stops worth planning your trip around

Mauritius is small enough to explore in a week, but every coast feels different. The north is lively and easy, the west has sunsets and mountain views, the south is green and dramatic, and the east is quiet, wide, and beach-focused.

This guide brings together the best places to visit in Mauritius with practical details tourists actually need: how long to spend, when to go, what kind of day to plan, and how each stop fits into a private MAUTRAVEL tour.

1

Le Morne Brabant

Rating4.9 / 5 Visit timeHalf day to full day Days to visit1 day with Chamarel or Black River

Le Morne is one of the most dramatic landscapes in Mauritius. The mountain rises behind a turquoise lagoon, and the whole area feels open, quiet, and cinematic. It is ideal for beach time, scenic photos, coastal drives, and learning about one of the island's most meaningful historic sites.

Best time: Clear mornings for photos, or late afternoon if you want softer sunset light.

Le Morne guide · West Coast Tour
2

Chamarel Seven Coloured Earth and Waterfall

Rating4.8 / 5 Visit time2 to 3 hours Days to visitBest on a south-west day tour

Chamarel shows a completely different side of Mauritius. The Seven Coloured Earth looks almost painted into the ground, while the waterfall drops into a deep green valley nearby. It is one of the easiest places to combine nature, viewpoints, local rum tasting, and relaxed sightseeing.

Best time: Weekday mornings are calmer, especially during school holidays.

Chamarel location guide · South Tour
3

Ile aux Cerfs

Rating4.8 / 5 Visit timeFull day Days to visit1 relaxed east coast day

Ile aux Cerfs is the classic Mauritius island excursion. The boat ride, shallow lagoon, soft beaches, water sports, snorkeling, and parasailing make it one of the most popular day trips for couples, families, and first-time visitors.

Best time: Choose a sunny, low-wind day and avoid rushing back too early.

4

Black River Gorges National Park

Rating4.7 / 5 Visit timeHalf day Days to visitHalf day or with Chamarel

Black River Gorges is Mauritius at its greenest. The roads feel more like mountain routes than island roads, with cooler air, forest viewpoints, valleys, and waterfalls. It is perfect for nature lovers and visitors who want something quieter than the beaches.

Best time: Clear mornings are best for viewpoints before clouds gather inland.

West Coast Tour
5

Grand Baie

Rating4.6 / 5 Visit timeHalf day to evening Days to visitHalf day with north coast stops

Grand Baie is the north's most popular resort area, with restaurants, shopping, beaches, nightlife, and boat departures. It is a strong base for travellers who want easy dining options and quick access to Pereybere, Mont Choisy, and Cap Malheureux.

Best time: Good any day, but livelier on weekends and evenings.

Grand Baie guide · North Tour
6

Pamplemousses Botanical Garden

Rating4.5 / 5 Visit time1.5 to 2 hours Days to visitEasy stop on a north tour

The Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Botanical Garden is calm, shaded, and easy to enjoy. Visitors usually come for the giant water lilies, palm avenues, old trees, and relaxed walking paths. It is especially good for families and slower sightseeing days.

Best time: Morning visits are cooler and more comfortable.

North Tour
7

Port Louis and Central Market

Rating4.5 / 5 Visit time2 to 4 hours Days to visitHalf day with Caudan and Fort Adelaide

Port Louis gives you the local rhythm of Mauritius. The Central Market is full of colours, spices, fruits, and street food, while Caudan Waterfront adds a more modern shopping and dining stop. Fort Adelaide is worth adding for a wide view of the harbour and city.

Best time: Weekday mornings are best for the market.

8

Flic en Flac Beach

Rating4.5 / 5 Visit timeHalf day or sunset evening Days to visitHalf day with Casela or Tamarin

Flic en Flac is one of the easiest west coast beaches to enjoy. The beach is long, the lagoon is calm, and the sunset often turns the whole shoreline golden. It is a relaxed choice after Casela, Tamarin, Black River, or a west coast sightseeing route.

Best time: Late afternoon is best if you want sunset photos.

Flic en Flac guide · Sunset Escape
9

Casela Nature Parks

Rating4.4 / 5 Visit timeHalf day to full day Days to visit1 adventure day or with Flic en Flac

Casela is a strong choice for families and adventure travellers. Depending on your plan, you can include safari-style drives, animal encounters, ziplines, quad biking, mountain scenery, and easy west-side connections.

Best time: Weekdays are better for shorter queues. Some activities need advance booking.

Casela guide · Casela Tour
10

Belle Mare and the East Coast

Rating4.4 / 5 Visit timeHalf day to full day Days to visit1 slow beach day

Belle Mare is long, open, and quieter than many beaches in the north and west. The east coast feels spacious and refined, with pale sand, luxury resorts, and a slower pace. It is the kind of place where doing less is the whole point.

Best time: Choose a calm, sunny day. The east coast can be windier than other areas.

FAQ

Top places in Mauritius questions

What is the number one place to visit in Mauritius?

Le Morne Brabant is one of the best first choices because it combines beach, mountain scenery, history, and west coast views in one stop.

How many days do I need to visit these places?

Five to seven days is enough for the main highlights, but ten days feels more relaxed if you want beaches, island excursions, and private tours.

Can I visit the top places without renting a car?

Yes. A private MAUTRAVEL tour or transfer is often easier because attractions are spread around the island and local route planning saves time.
