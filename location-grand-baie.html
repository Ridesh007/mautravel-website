North Mauritius

Grand Baie

Grand Baie is one of Mauritius' best bases for beaches, restaurants, shopping, nightlife, and northern island excursions.

Book a Taxi to Grand Baie

Beach time

Visit Grand Baie beach, Pereybere, Mont Choisy, or La Cuvette for calm swimming and easy photos.

Shopping

Explore boutiques, malls, beachwear stores, local crafts, and souvenir shops.

Dining and nightlife

Enjoy seafood restaurants, sunset drinks, nightlife, and relaxed resort-area evenings.

Recommended plan

  • Morning pickup from your hotel and coastal drive to the north.
  • Photo stop at Cap Malheureux before lunch in Grand Baie.
  • Afternoon beach time or shopping, then sunset restaurant drop-off.
