Most visitors can drive in Mauritius on their home country licence for up to 90 days. An International Driving Permit is recommended for non-English and non-French licences. We advise checking your specific licence requirements when you enquire.

Mauritius drives on the left, the same as the UK, India, and Australia. Steering wheels are on the right side. Roads are generally well-maintained with clear signage in English and French.
