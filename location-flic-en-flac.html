West coast beach

Flic en Flac

Flic en Flac is a long west-coast beach loved for swimming, sunsets, restaurants, snorkeling spots, and relaxed evenings by the sea.

Book a Taxi to Flic en Flac

Beach activities

Swim, walk the long beach, relax under casuarina trees, and enjoy west-coast lagoon views.

Snorkeling

Bring reef-safe sunscreen and ask your driver for safe, easy beach access points.

Sunset and dining

Stay for golden-hour photos, then head to nearby restaurants for a relaxed evening.
