Best Time to Visit Mauritius

How to think about timing for beaches, tours, sunsets, and attraction days.

May to December

This period is often preferred for comfortable touring conditions, beach days, and full-day sightseeing. Temperatures are usually easier for long taxi tours, making it a good match for South Tours, mountain routes, and West Coast Tours.

Warmer summer months

Summer feels more tropical and humid, which can be beautiful for lagoon days and bright beach colors. Plan early starts, keep water with you, and allow flexibility for passing showers, especially around mountain routes.

Best sunset areas

West coast areas such as Flic en Flac, Tamarin, Black River, and Le Morne are excellent for sunset-focused trips. A private taxi makes it easier to time dinner drop-off after photos.

Best family timing

For families, start attractions early, avoid overpacked itineraries, and keep a flexible return time. Routes such as Casela, Pamplemousses Garden, or light beach days are easier when children need breaks.
