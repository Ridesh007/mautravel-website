Port Louis

Port Louis gives visitors markets, street food, museums, the Caudan Waterfront, cultural landmarks, and a lively city contrast to beach days.

Book a Taxi to Port Louis

Central Market

Browse spices, fruit, crafts, textiles, and street snacks in the city's most active market.

Caudan Waterfront

Enjoy shops, restaurants, waterfront views, museums, and easy walking areas.

Culture

Visit museums, old streets, temples, churches, and heritage buildings with local context.

Recommended plan

  • Start early to avoid city traffic and enjoy cooler market hours.
  • Combine Port Louis with Pamplemousses Botanical Garden or north coast beaches.
  • Great for shopping, food lovers, cultural sightseeing, and half-day city tours.
