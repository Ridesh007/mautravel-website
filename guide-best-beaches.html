Best Beaches in Mauritius

Where to swim, take photos, watch sunset, and plan relaxed beach transfers.

Flic en Flac

Best for sunsets, long walks, swimming, restaurants, and relaxed west coast evenings. It is a practical choice for travelers who want beach time without a complicated route, and it pairs well with Casela, Tamarin, or Black River.

Flic en Flac guide

Le Morne

Best for dramatic mountain scenery, honeymoon photos, kitesurfing views, and south-west lagoon landscapes. It is one of the most photogenic beach areas in Mauritius.

Le Morne guide

Ile aux Cerfs

Best for boat transfers, lagoon beaches, snorkeling, parasailing, and east coast adventure. This is a strong option when guests want the day to feel like a small island escape.

Ile aux Cerfs guide

Mont Choisy

Best for spacious sand, calm shade, easy family time, and north coast touring. It works well as a relaxed stop during a Grand Baie and Cap Malheureux day.

North Tour

Pereybere

Best for swimming near Grand Baie, casual food stops, and easy short beach visits. It is convenient if you are staying in the north or planning shopping and dining nearby.

Grand Baie guide

Belle Mare

Best for long east coast beach scenery, resort transfers, sunrise colors, and open lagoon views. It can be combined with an Ile aux Cerfs transfer or east coast sightseeing.
