Mauritius Food Guide

What to try and where to plan easy food stops during your tour.

Street food

Try dholl puri, farata, gateaux piments, boulettes, mine frite, and fresh fruit. Port Louis is one of the easiest places to experience the island's everyday food culture during a city stop.

Port Louis food stops

Beach dining

Grand Baie, Flic en Flac, and Le Morne are strong options for seafood, casual beach meals, sunset dinners, and resort-area restaurants. They are convenient choices after a beach or shopping stop.

Grand Baie guide

Tour lunches

South and west routes can include lunch near Chamarel, Black River, Flic en Flac, or local village restaurants. Ask for timing that gives enough room for sightseeing without rushing the meal.
