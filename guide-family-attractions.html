Family Attractions in Mauritius

Kid-friendly ideas for relaxed private taxi days with beaches, parks, and flexible stops.

Casela Nature Parks

Casela is one of the island's easiest family adventure days, with safari-style viewing, optional adventure activities, open landscapes, and enough variety to keep children engaged.

Casela guide · Casela Tour

Easy beaches

Flic en Flac, Mont Choisy, Pereybere, and Belle Mare work well for family beach stops because they can be combined with food breaks, short transfers, and flexible return times.

Best beaches guide

Botanical Garden

The Pamplemousses Botanical Garden offers gentle paths, tropical plants, giant water lilies, shade, and calmer pacing for children, parents, and grandparents.

North Tour

Vallee des Couleurs

Vallee des Couleurs is good for older children and active families who want ziplines, viewpoints, waterfalls, bridge walks, quad biking options, and more nature-based activities.

Vallee des Couleurs Tour

Private family taxi

A private family taxi keeps the day easier: request child seats, plan comfort breaks, avoid overpacking the route, and keep the schedule flexible around lunch and beach time.

Family Adventure Package

Trou aux Cerfs

Trou aux Cerfs is a simple and scenic family stop where visitors can see the volcanic crater, enjoy cooler central-island air, take photos, and combine the visit with Curepipe or a south tour route.
