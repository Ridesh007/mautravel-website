Travel Tips for Mauritius

Small choices that make airport transfers, private tours, and beach days smoother.

Book transfers early

Book airport transfers early and share your flight number so pickup timing is easier to coordinate. This matters for late arrivals, early departures, family groups, and guests carrying several suitcases.

Send a booking request

Group places by region

Plan north with Grand Baie, south with Chamarel, east with Ile aux Cerfs, and west with Flic en Flac. This saves transfer time and makes each day feel less rushed.

View tour packages

Carry some cash

Carry cash for small shops, snacks, tips, markets, and some attraction counters. Cards are useful, but small local stops can still be easier with Mauritian rupees.

Grand Baie guide

Confirm what is included

Ask whether entrance fees, boat rides, paid activities, and lunch are included before confirming a tour. This is especially important for Ile aux Cerfs, Casela, and Vallee des Couleurs.

East Coast Adventure

Families need flexibility

For families, request child seats and choose flexible schedules with shorter transfer blocks. A calm garden, one attraction, and one beach often works better than a packed checklist.

Family Adventure Package

Leave room for weather

Plan mountain and south-west routes with a little flexibility, especially around Chamarel, Black River, and Le Morne. Passing showers can happen, but the scenery is often worth the wait.
